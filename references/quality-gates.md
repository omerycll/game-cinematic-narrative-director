# Quality gates and red-team evaluation

Score each 0-5 with evidence and a required change if score <=3. Weighted /100 (rating/5 * weight):
1. First 30 sec hook (8)
2. Original human stakes and relationships (12)
3. Internal truth and chronology (14)
4. Fair clues and delayed revelations (12)
5. Player agency and choice callbacks (15)
6. Puzzle integration and solvability (10)
7. Dread rhythm and emotional contrast (10)
8. Visual / sound signature (8)
9. Indie scope, asset reuse, localization (6)
10. Accessibility, save, replay UX (5)

Rubric output is editorial, not a statistically validated score. Target >=85 for **prototype candidate**, never a guarantee. Add a 5-item failure log with original failure, fix, and evidence; verify fixes against scene atlas, chapter graph and clue ledger.

## Structural gates
- Every graph node reachable; all advertised endings realizable; no unreachable choices.
- Every major choice observable again later in scene/NPC state, not just a counter.
- Story twist has >=2 earlier source-clued observations that survive chronology review.
- Every protagonist action is motivated and justified by the player-visible situation.
- Ending resolution respects character agency, consent, and established threat limits.
- Planned first-run duration is path-based, replay delta separately enumerated.
- Every scene has specific verbs, objects, exit and art variants; not only a plot summary.
- Each puzzle has 3 hints and feedback for wrong inputs; no mandatory audio/color-only signal.
- Check low-end phone touch targets, time to skip repeated scenes, autosave, and chapter reset.
