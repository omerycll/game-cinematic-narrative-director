# Duration and indie production economics

## Playtime model
For each scene separately estimate: `inspection_minutes`, `puzzle_minutes`, `dialogue_minutes`, `traversal_minutes`, `optional_minutes`; sum for target route. Distinguish planned central times from fastest familiar-user route and slower first discovery. Reused dialogue and solved puzzles shrink replay time: report that honestly.

## Multi-hour narrative minimum
A ~3-4h first playthrough usually needs dozens of meaningful playable sequences, but exact count depends on puzzle complexity and reading speed. Do not add filler locks or force repeated walks. Sample allocation: 5 acts / 28-34 production scenes, roughly 35-50 planned min per act, first-run path ~190-230 minutes, alternate routes and endings. These are pre-playtest production estimates, not guarantees.

## Art scope checkpoint
Recommended solo scope starting point: 10-14 unique room backgrounds, 20-35 lighting/state changes, 3-5 character portrait/sprite families, 8-14 principal puzzles, 40-80 hotspot variants and 4 distinct outcomes. If scripting 30 scenes in 12 rooms, annotate every revisit state and why it feels new.

## MVP gate
Build and playtest one complete 20-30 minute segment with 3 rooms, 2 puzzles, 2 human interactions, 1 choice that changes a later room state, 1 substantial revelation and a full save/reload pass. Measure actual times; revise the entire project's duration claim from telemetry or manually recorded pilot timings.

## Release minimum
Completable without consulting walkthrough; save and restart stable; translations fit; skip seen cutscenes; subtle hints escalate; no audio-only bottlenecks; art click targets tested on small phones; no mystery depends on guesswork.
